Our Services

Comprehensive consulting services designed to take your infrastructure projects from concept to completion with excellence and efficiency.

  • Submittals

    Prepare, organize and track submittal packages against the contract documents and specifications, including logs, 2 resubmittals and approval follow-ups.

  • Scheduling

    Baseline and updated CPM schedules and short look-ahead for the field, built from the drawings and kept in line with real conditions.

  • Estimating

    Quantity takeoffs, cost estimates and budgets for bidding and for tracking cost against budget once the job starts.

  • Procurement and Buyout Support

    A procurement schedule from your drawings and specs with long-lead items flagged early, quote requests, leveled bid comparisons and a weekly status report showing what is ordered, late or at risk.

  • Project Management

    Planning, document control, RFI and meeting follow-up, using MS Project, Bluebeam and Procore, to back up your project team.

  • Residential Project Support

    Quantity takeoffs and BOQs, budgets, construction schedules, contractor and material quote comparison, drawing review and progress tracking for homes, apartments and housing projects.
